About

I work on geotechnical site characterisation, using statistical and machine-learning methods to build reliable ground models from limited investigation data. My focus is on linear infrastructure, where boreholes are sparse and lie along a single line.

I am a member of the Geo Innovation Research Laboratory at Chonnam National University.

Research

General research interests

  • Site characterisation

    Building continuous soil profiles from sparse borehole and in-situ test data.

  • Spatial variability

    Modelling correlation of soil properties and quantifying uncertainty in the ground.

  • State-space and geostatistical models

    Probabilistic methods that predict soil properties and layering together with calibrated uncertainty.

  • Machine learning in geotechnics

    Data-driven prediction of soil type and strength, and how it compares with classical methods.

  • Underground and linear infrastructure

    Ground models for tunnels, subways and other corridor projects.
