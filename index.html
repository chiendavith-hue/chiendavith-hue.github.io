About

I earned a Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering from Paragon International University, Cambodia, in 2024. Before joining the integrated Master's–Ph.D. program, I worked as a Geotechnical Junior Engineer, with experience in geotechnical data analysis, excavation monitoring and geotechnical modelling.

I am a member of the Geo Innovation Research Laboratory at Chonnam National University.

Research

Research focus: geomaterial development and deep excavations

  • Geomaterial development

    Characterising and developing geomaterials for geotechnical applications.

  • Deep excavations

    Excavation behaviour and monitoring, drawing on field experience in geotechnical practice.

  • Site characterisation

    Building continuous soil profiles from sparse borehole and in-situ test data, and quantifying spatial variability.

  • Machine learning in geotechnics

    Data-driven prediction of soil behaviour, and how spatial information is encoded in the models.

  • State-space and geostatistical models

    Probabilistic ground models that give predictions with calibrated uncertainty.
